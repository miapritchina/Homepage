About this collection

Paintings come from museums that publish open-access images: the Met, Cleveland, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Smithsonian's Freer Gallery, and, through Wikimedia Commons, Taipei's National Palace Museum, Princeton, Yale, Minneapolis, Indianapolis and others. Images are shown from the museums' own servers where they offer zoom, or as tiles cut from the open-access files.

Museums that allow no copies, such as the Palace Museum in Beijing and the Shanghai Museum, appear as link-only cards.

Schools, approach and subject tags are assigned automatically from titles, media and painter. Painter notes are short summaries. Treat both as a guide, not a catalogue.
