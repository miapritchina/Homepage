Studio · blending

Smudge gradients

Ink painters grade colour with a second, damp brush (分染). In Procreate the smudge tool does the same job. Three rules keep it from ruining texture and line:

  1. Smudge only touches the selected layer. Keep colour on Plant colour 水色 and lines on Lines 勾, and your strokes are never at risk.
  2. The paper is safe. In every canvas the paper texture sits on its own locked layer at the top, so smudging below it never wipes the grain.
  3. Use a grainy smudge brush at low strength. A soft, smooth brush at full opacity is what turns a blend into plastic.

A two-colour gradient

1Block in two colours side by side

Paint the two colours so they touch, for example rouge at a petal tip and cinnabar toward the base. Don’t try to blend yet.

Wet and dry, digitally

Wet = paint on the same layer. Wet Mix brushes pick up and blend with what is there, like ink into damp paper (破墨).

Dry = move to the next layer up, or add a new layer just above in the same group. Strokes stack without merging, like waiting for ink to dry (积墨).

The canvas layers

Every canvas opens with these layers, top to bottom. Each step below names the layer it goes on.

  1. Paper 纸PaperLinear Burn, locked. Gives every stroke the paper’s grain.
  2. Line · accentsSeal 印The red seal, last of all.
  3. Line · accentsDots 点Moss dots, stamens, darkest accents.
  4. Line · accentsLines 勾Outlines and contour line.
  5. Colour 色Mineral colour 石色Normal. Opaque azurite, malachite, cinnabar, shell white.
  6. Colour 色Plant colour 水色Multiply. Transparent glazes and 分染 shading.
  7. Ink wash 墨Texture 皴擦Cun and rubbing over the washes.
  8. Ink wash 墨Washes 染Big masses, washes, broken ink.

In xieyi you can paint masses and lines together on Washes 染 so they melt into each other. Move to a separate layer when you want control.

Four brush habits

  • Tilt brushes widen when the pencil is laid low, like a brush on its side.
  • 2-colour brushes blend your primary and secondary colour swatches: dark one for the edge, light one inside.
  • [Smudge] brushes only work from the smudge tool.
  • Names with LIGHTLY or LIGHT touch = DENSE have inverted pressure.

Paper

Forming a sheet on the screen: museum diorama of xuan papermaking.

Xuan (宣纸) is made in Jing County, Anhui, from qingtan bark and rice straw. The craft takes over a hundred steps and is on UNESCO's heritage list.

"Rice paper" is a misleading name. The bark is what makes it xuan: its long fibres give the fine, hair-like feathering at the edge of a wet stroke.

For your brushesEvery ink brush in the pack will need a raw-paper and a sized-paper version. The paper changes the stroke more than the brush does.

Anatomy of a halo

2.15 Water Runs Ink Stays (SX001.2): black core, grey carbon, pale water front with a ragged tide line.

Water runs ahead, carbon lags. Ink is soot particles 10–150 nm across, suspended in glue water. The paper's fibres wick the water outward; the soot is carried along but falls behind.

The edge darkens. Where the wet front stops, evaporation pulls more particles to the rim, the same effect as a coffee ring.

Researchers name the effects to copy: feathery patterns, light fringes, branching, rough and darkened boundaries.

Colour

Colour on ink

Chinese painting colour rests on about ten materials, and it always sits on an ink structure. Choose a palette by the kind of painting, not by the subject.

  • 水
    Plant colours are transparentIndigo, gamboge, rouge. They sink into the paper, glaze, and mix with each other.
  • 石
    Mineral colours are opaqueAzurite, malachite, cinnabar. They sit on top, cover what is beneath, and are laid over a plant-colour underlayer.
  • 墨
    Ink comes firstColour supports the ink drawing. A painting that works in ink alone works with colour too.

Swatches are illustrations for Procreate, not measurements. Published hex values for the same name often disagree.

Layering colour

Minerals are never laid straight onto paper. Each goes over a transparent underlayer of related colour:

  • Azurite over indigo.
  • Malachite over indigo + gamboge, or ochre.
  • Cinnabar over rouge or a light red.

In gongbi three kinds of wash build each shape: 分染 grades one colour from dark to clear, 罩染 glazes a thin colour over the whole shape, and 统染 unifies a whole area at the end.

In ProcreateUnderlayer and 分染 on Multiply layers below the line. Minerals on a Normal layer above them, with an opaque grainy brush. See Into Procreate for the full layer stack.

Restraint

Blank paper (留白) counts as a colour. Mature work uses fewer hues, not more.

Rules painters repeated

  • 红间绿，花簇簇 Red next to green makes flowers burst out.
  • 青间紫，不如死 Blue next to purple looks dead: keep neighbours of similar darkness apart.
  • The seal is the last accent. One small spot of red, placed to balance the composition.
  • Pitfalls in gifts. White is a mourning colour, and a green hat on a man implies infidelity.

Old paintings have changed colour

Lead white and red lead darken to brown-black, as on many Dunhuang murals. Rouge, gamboge and indigo fade. When you copy an old painting, decide whether you paint the colour it has now or the colour it had.

The five colours

Classical theory names five correct colours, each tied to a direction and season: 青 east and spring, 赤 south and summer, 黄 centre, 白 west and autumn, 黑 north and winter. Mixed colours such as green and purple were "intermediate". 青 itself covers blue, green and even black.

Before you rely on a number

Caveats

  1. Recipes vary. Bark ratios, glue-to-alum ratios and grade numbering differ by maker and source. Micron values are approximate.
  2. Some claims are commercial. Pine-versus-oil gradient character and several how-to details come from retailers or popular articles.
  3. Orpiment was rare. Don't present it as a staple yellow.
  4. Back-painting evidence is thin beyond the conservation studies cited.
  5. Humidity and pressure curves weren't measured. Those suggestions are reasoned, not tested.
  6. Stroke pictures are brush previews, not scans of real ink. They show what your digital brushes do, which is the target to compare with real ink.

About the files

Notes on the English names

  • 2.11 Round Light Ink says "even mid washes", but the stroke is a fuzzy cloudy mass and can go dark.
  • 1.12 Overnight Ink (Sumo): "sumo" is just the pinyin of 宿墨, overnight ink.
  • 3.1 Chuncai-Strip Line (莼菜条) is one of the classic 18 figure-line styles, named after water-shield stems.
