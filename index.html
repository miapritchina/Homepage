  1. 01

    Paintwheel

    A real-time wet-watercolour simulator running in the browser. Pigment blooms, feathers and backruns into wet paper, and dries to crisp darkened edges — a fluid model from the watercolour graphics literature, run as WebGL2 shader passes. Paint with a mouse, a finger, or a pressure-sensitive pen.

    • WebGL2
    • Fluid sim
    • PWA
    • 2026

    Guqin Finger Techniques古琴指法

    A searchable reference for the hand techniques of the guqin as they appear in jiǎnzìpǔ tablature: each one with its reduced glyph, pinyin, plain instruction, the poetic gesture-image it was taught by, and sources.

    Kaleidoscope

    Shards tumbling behind mirrored slices, drawn on canvas. The number of slices, the palette and the rotation speed are all yours to push around; pause on a pattern you like and save it out as a PNG.

  6. 06

    The Music Room

    A practice studio for guitar, ukulele and piano: chord voicings and capo positions, scales from the pentatonics through harmonic minor to Chinese modes, a melody sketcher, sight-reading and ear-training drills, and a running tally of minutes practised on each instrument.

    • Music
    • React
    • Practice tool
    • 2026
    Open Source
  7. 07

    SoundReWave

    Sing or play into the microphone and the take is drawn as a pitch contour, stacked over the ones before it so you can read one attempt against the last. Mic sensitivity and the detection threshold are adjustable, a playhead scrubs the stack, and the graph exports as PNG or the takes as WAV.

    • Web Audio
    • Pitch detection
    • TypeScript
    • 2026
    Open Source
